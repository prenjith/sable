# Observer rubric

Record one row per task. Use the CSV template.

- `success`: 1 if the task met the criteria, 0 otherwise.
- `time_seconds`: from reading the prompt to finishing or stopping.
- `attempts`: number of runs before success or stop.
- `friction`: 1 if the participant got stuck, needed a hint, or hit a language
  limitation; 0 otherwise.
- `confidence_before` / `confidence_after`: from the survey.
- `notes`: one or two short observations, no names.

Also capture:

- Time to first successful run.
- Error types seen (syntax, type, runtime).
- Features used without help.
- Two direct quotes per session, with permission.

Do not coach syntax unless the participant has been stuck for three minutes.
Record the hint when you give one.
