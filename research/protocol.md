# Session protocol

## Setup

- One facilitator and one observer if possible.
- Browser playground open, or a native binary.
- Task cards printed or on screen.
- CSV open for notes, or paper that you transcribe later.
- Water, quiet space, no time pressure.

## 1. Welcome and consent (5 min)

- Introduce yourself and why you are testing Sable.
- Say clearly: "We are testing the language, not you. There are no wrong
  answers. You can stop at any time."
- Collect guardian consent and participant assent if under 18.
- Explain the anonymous ID and what will be recorded.

## 2. Warm-up (5 min)

Ask the participant to make Sable print a greeting. Do not explain syntax unless
they ask; point at the playground examples instead.

Success: one line of output.

## 3. Tasks (12 min)

Pick two or three cards from `tasks.md` based on the participant's goal. Read
each prompt aloud. Ask them to think out loud.

Rules:

- Let them struggle for up to three minutes before giving a hint.
- Record the hint and the friction.
- Stop a task after eight minutes and mark it incomplete.
- Move on even if the task is unfinished.

## 4. Wrap-up (3 min)

- Post-survey.
- Ask: "What was the most confusing part?" and "What would you build with this?"
- Thank them and remind them they can ask for their data to be deleted.

## After the session

- Fill one CSV row per task.
- Write down two or three direct quotes.
- Do not add names, emails, or school details.
