# Pre/post survey

Keep it short. A five-point scale is enough.

## Before

1. How much coding have you done before? (1 = none, 5 = a lot)
2. How confident do you feel about trying a new language? (1–5)
3. What would you like to build? (open text)

## After

1. How confident do you feel about Sable now? (1–5)
2. Which part was easiest?
3. Which part was hardest?
4. Would you use Sable again for a small project? (yes / maybe / no)
5. Anything else? (open text)

Do not ask for names. Link the answers to the participant ID only.
