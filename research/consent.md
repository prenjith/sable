# Consent and assent templates

These are templates, not legal advice. Follow your school, club, and Hack Club
safeguarding rules. For participants under 18, get guardian consent and the
participant's own assent.

## Guardian consent (template)

I give permission for my child to take part in a usability study of the Sable
programming language.

- The session takes about 25 minutes.
- The study is about the language, not about my child's ability.
- Participation is voluntary. My child can stop at any time.
- The study records an anonymous participant ID, task success, timing, attempts,
  confidence ratings, and short notes.
- No names, emails, school names, or code are stored in the shared dataset.
- Data is stored locally by the organizer and shared only as aggregate results.
- We can ask for the data to be deleted at any time.

Guardian name: ______________________

Signature: __________________________  Date: ______________

## Participant assent (template)

I agree to try Sable and answer a few questions.

- I can stop whenever I want.
- It is okay if I do not finish a task.
- The organizer will write down how the tasks went, but not my name.
- I can ask them to delete my data.

Name: _______________________________

Signature: __________________________  Date: ______________

## Data handling

- Use IDs like `AUT-01`, not names.
- Keep the CSV and notes on a device you control.
- Do not put the CSV in a public repository.
- Delete the data if a participant or guardian asks.
