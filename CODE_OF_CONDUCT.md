# Code of conduct

Sable follows the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect different levels of experience.
- Do not harass, insult, or exclude people.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory jokes.
- Sexual attention or imagery.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate
this code. Report problems privately to the maintainer listed in the repository
profile.

The full Contributor Covenant 2.1 text is at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
